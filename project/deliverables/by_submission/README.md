# Deliverables organized by submission

These folders are navigation covers. They point to the authoritative files in the repository so results are not duplicated or allowed to drift out of sync.

## Submission sequence

| Submission | Weight in guide | Additions to the continuing design record | Navigation cover |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Intermediate 1 | 10% | About two pages: proposal, sources, roles, interfaces, minimum case | [`01_intermediate_1/README.md`](01_intermediate_1/README.md) |
| Intermediate 2 | 15% | Expand to about three-four pages: verification, detectors, behavior, preliminary pathway | [`02_intermediate_2/README.md`](02_intermediate_2/README.md) |
| Intermediate 3 | 20% | Add observation, forecast, exchange, basic TMC, prototype | [`03_intermediate_3/README.md`](03_intermediate_3/README.md) |
| Final technical submission | 25% | Six-page main report plus references/plots/code and executable Pathway A system | [`04_final/README.md`](04_final/README.md) |

The guide separately assigns 15% to individual progress presentations and 15% to the final presentation/project questions. Technical scores are 60% shared evidence and 40% individual work; this is an individual project, so the contribution record identifies the full coherent component you own.

## Files that accompany the project throughout

- [`../../README.md`](../../README.md): reproducible command, dependencies, inputs, outputs, and limitations.
- [`../design_record.md`](../design_record.md): the single evolving design record.
- [`../interfaces.md`](../interfaces.md): stable component exchange formats.
- [`../source_to_design_table.md`](../source_to_design_table.md): source-to-equation/parameter/code traceability.
- [`../../outputs/results/experiment_record.csv`](../../outputs/results/experiment_record.csv): prediction, change, result, interpretation.
- [`../individual_contribution_record.md`](../individual_contribution_record.md): named ownership, tests, and conclusions.
- [`../ai_use_and_verification.md`](../ai_use_and_verification.md): concise AI-use and human-verification disclosure.

## What the instructor is likely to assess every time

1. Is the scope appropriate and is the implementation real rather than only discussed?
2. Is there a check that could reveal a coding/model error?
3. Are comparisons fair and are negative results reported honestly?
4. Can you connect a source to an equation, the equation to code, and code to a result?
5. Can you personally explain the design decision and its limitations?
